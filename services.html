INSTALLATION & SUPPORT DOMAINS

Practical installation and maintenance solutions tailored to distinct architectural formats.

Residential CCTV Installation

Tailored camera layouts for independent houses, villas, and apartment residences. We address property boundaries, entrance gates, porch verandas, and garage bays while safeguarding family and neighbor privacy.

  • Strategic main gate & perimeter camera positioning
  • Concealed conduit cabling respecting home decor
  • Secure local NVR/DVR setup with display monitor connection
  • Authorized mobile remote access demonstration

Commercial CCTV Installation

Surveillance engineering designed for corporate office complexes, retail showrooms, restaurants, and warehouses requiring multi-camera continuous coverage and staff safety monitoring.

  • Cash counter, POS register & transaction monitoring
  • Reception entry, elevator lobbies & emergency staircase exits
  • Loading dock & stockroom inventory zone coverage
  • Multi-channel rack-mount recorder commissioning

IP Camera & Network Deployment

Structured Cat6 data cabling deployment, RJ45 crimping, Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) switch balancing, and digital IP camera network integration.

  • Cat6 structured Ethernet cable routing with label tagging
  • PoE switch power budget assessment per camera line
  • Dedicated camera subnet configuration avoiding LAN congestion
  • Comprehensive stream testing and client handover

DVR & NVR Installation

Physical mounting of recording units into secure lockboxes or server racks, surveillance hard drive formatting, camera channel mapping, and retention rules.

  • Surveillance-rated HDD installation and storage sector health check
  • Motion trigger zones and continuous recording schedule setups
  • HDMI and VGA local monitor output connectivity
  • Step-by-step playback search and clip export demonstration

Remote Viewing Setup

Assisting authorized property owners in configuring secure smartphone and desktop client software to check live camera feeds and review archived alerts.

  • Authorized mobile application pairing for iOS and Android
  • Administrator vs. guest user access tier configuration
  • Guidance on strong password protection & biometric app locks
  • Basic end-user tutorial on remote camera switching

CCTV Repair & Troubleshooting

Systematic diagnostics for existing security camera setups experiencing video blackouts, flickering feeds, recording failures, or network dropouts.

  • Investigating camera video loss ("No Video" errors)
  • Power supply SMPS and PoE port voltage verification
  • BNC connector, DC jack, and RJ45 re-termination
  • Storage drive read/write failure checks and recovery advice

CCTV Upgrades & Expansion

Modernizing outdated low-resolution analog surveillance systems to crisp high-definition or digital IP network cameras with expanded storage capacities.

  • Analog-to-IP migration using hybrid DVR/NVR recorders
  • Adding new camera channels to cover newly renovated areas
  • Storage capacity upgrades for longer retention windows
  • Full phased legacy system replacement planning

Annual Maintenance (AMC)

Routine preventative maintenance visits ensuring all camera housings remain free of dirt and cobwebs, cables remain firmly seated, and storage drives record uninterruptedly.

  • Periodic optical lens & protective glass cleaning
  • Cable connection and weather seal inspection
  • Hard drive health evaluation (S.M.A.R.T. logging)
  • Recorder time synchronization & firmware checks
PREVENTATIVE CARE

ILLUSTRATIVE AMC MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST

Sample maintenance activities recommended during scheduled preventative visits to guarantee optimal sensor focus, weather seal protection, and recording continuity.

1. Camera & Lens Cleaning

Gentle removal of dust, road exhaust residue, spider webs, and moisture film from front glass elements to maintain clear day and night focus.

2. Cabling & Junction Inspection

Verifying physical conduits, junction box water seals, and RJ45/BNC connector crimps against weather exposure and corrosion.

3. Power Delivery Assessment

Inspecting power adapters, SMPS voltage output, and PoE switch load margins to avoid sudden reboot loops or signal dropouts.

4. Recording Verification

Auditing historical recording timelines to confirm motion events and continuous recordings are reliably logged on the hard drives.

5. Storage Health Review

Scanning hard drive bad sectors, operational temperature logs, and automatic overwrite functionality across all installed storage media.

6. Angle Alignment & Clock Sync

Re-aligning cameras shifted by wind or tree growth, and synchronizing system time with network NTP servers for accurate legal timestamps.

THE 6-STEP INSTALLATION PROCESS

An illustrative workflow demonstrating how we transition from initial property review to final customer handover.

01

Initial Enquiry

We review your property type, approximate dimensions, and security objectives to formulate an initial camera count recommendation.

02

Property Site Survey

03

IMPORTANT PRIVACY & SAFETY COMMITMENT

Security camera systems should be planned thoughtfully to respect individual privacy and comply with applicable local laws:

  • Never place surveillance cameras in restrooms, private bedrooms, or areas where personal privacy is reasonably expected.
  • Carefully adjust camera viewing angles so lenses do not unintentionally capture neighbouring windows, private courtyards, or balconies.
  • Safeguard recorder hardware, administrative passwords, and network routers against unauthorized physical or digital tampering.
  • In commercial premises, display prominent CCTV signage informing visitors and staff of active video monitoring as required by local authorities.
