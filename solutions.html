SURVEILLANCE SYSTEMS & EQUIPMENT

EXPLORE THE RIGHT CAMERA SYSTEM FOR YOUR PROPERTY.

Compare camera architectures, transmission technologies, housing form factors, and recording options to find the ideal match for your residential or commercial site.

Coaxial Video Architecture

HD CCTV Camera Systems

Analog High-Definition systems transmit uncompressed video over coaxial RG59 or 3+1 composite cabling directly to a digital video recorder (DVR). Delivering zero transmission delay and straightforward plug-and-play operation, they are a practical choice for budget-conscious upgrades of existing cable infrastructure.

Suitable Property Type: Single-family homes, independent shops, small offices, and retrofits with existing coaxial wires.
Key Considerations: Requires physical point-to-point wiring from each camera directly back to the DVR; power supply distribution boxes needed near recorder.
Request HD CCTV Enquiry
Digital Network Architecture

IP Camera Systems

Network IP cameras process image data directly on the sensor before transmitting digital video streams over standard Cat6 Ethernet cabling. Utilizing Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) switches and Network Video Recorders (NVR), IP systems deliver high megapixel clarity, advanced digital zoom, and remote accessibility across local and wide-area networks.

Suitable Property Type: Modern residences, commercial buildings, corporate offices, apartment communities, and multi-building facilities.
Key Considerations: Requires Cat6 structured cabling, Gigabit PoE network switches, and sufficient network bandwidth capacity to support concurrent streams.
Request IP System Enquiry
Wi-Fi & Mesh Connectivity

Wireless CCTV Systems

Wireless security cameras transmit video signals over 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi frequencies without requiring long network cabling runs through interior walls. While signal transmission is wireless, these cameras generally require connection to a nearby electrical outlet or dedicated power adapter, as well as dependable Wi-Fi signal coverage throughout the installation perimeter.

Suitable Property Type: Finished apartments, historic homes, rental premises, and outbuildings where structural conduit drilling is prohibited.
Key Considerations: Wireless cameras still require reliable local power outlets; Wi-Fi network strength and potential interference must be verified before mounting.
Request Wireless CCTV Enquiry
Ceiling & Indoor Form Factor

Dome Cameras

Housed within a protective hemispherical glass or polycarbonate dome, these cameras blend smoothly into ceiling architecture. Their discreet design makes it challenging for onlookers to identify exactly where the lens is pointed, providing subtle presence suitable for public-facing customer areas, retail counters, and corporate conference spaces.

Suitable Property Type: Retail stores, restaurants, reception areas, elevators, lobbies, and indoor residential hallways.
Key Considerations: Best mounted on flat ceilings or soffits; dome covers require periodic dust cleaning to prevent infrared reflection glare during night recording.
Request Dome Camera Enquiry
Directional Outdoor Form Factor

Bullet Cameras

Featuring an elongated cylindrical shape, bullet cameras are engineered primarily for outdoor perimeter monitoring. Their visible profile acts as an overt visual deterrent, and their housing allows larger optical lenses capable of focusing across longer distances such as property boundary lines, exterior gates, and vehicle driveways.

Suitable Property Type: Building perimeters, parking lots, warehouse yards, gates, and exterior residential facades.
Key Considerations: Weather-resistant protective housing and sealed cable glands are required; specific environmental ratings should be confirmed against local climate needs.
Request Bullet Camera Enquiry
Pan • Tilt • Zoom Motorized Optics

PTZ Cameras

Pan-Tilt-Zoom cameras incorporate motorized mechanical gear assemblies that allow users or automated tour presets to rotate 360 degrees, tilt vertically, and magnify distant objects with powerful optical zoom lenses. They provide expansive dynamic coverage across wide open areas without requiring dozens of fixed cameras.

Suitable Property Type: Industrial yards, large logistics distribution centers, vehicle parking grounds, and expansive agricultural estates.
Key Considerations: Requires higher power draw (often Hi-PoE or dedicated power lines) and sturdy corner/pole mounting brackets capable of supporting motorized weight.
Request PTZ System Enquiry
Low-Light & Infrared Sensors

Night Vision Cameras

Equipped with smart infrared (IR) LED arrays or ultra-sensitive low-light CMOS sensors, night vision cameras automatically transition from full-color daytime capture to high-contrast night modes when ambient illumination drops. Smart IR automatically adjusts LED intensity to prevent close-up subjects from appearing washed out.

Suitable Property Type: Unlit alleys, nighttime storage facilities, perimeter fences, outdoor driveways, and after-hours offices.
Key Considerations: Effective night-time viewing distance depends on sensor quality and ambient lighting; image quality varies naturally across different lighting environments.
Request Night Vision Enquiry
Centralized Storage & Playback

DVR & NVR Recording Systems

The heart of any surveillance architecture is the recording unit. Digital Video Recorders (DVRs) encode incoming analog video signals, while Network Video Recorders (NVRs) record direct digital IP streams over Ethernet. Recorders feature dedicated SATA hard drives engineered for 24/7 continuous write cycles, scheduled motion recording, and fast footage export.

Suitable Property Type: All installations requiring organized multi-camera footage retention and local playback consoles.
Key Considerations: Total recording retention duration depends on video resolution, compression codec (e.g. H.265), frame rates, number of active cameras, and total drive capacity (TB).
