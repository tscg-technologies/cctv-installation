FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CCTV INSTALLATION & SURVEY FAQS

Common considerations when planning a security camera installation.

An on-site survey allows technicians to inspect building architecture, wall construction materials, conduit pathways, potential electromagnetic interference, and natural lighting variations between day and night. This prevents blind spots and ensures cabling can be routed neatly and safely.
Analog HD cameras transmit video over coaxial cables to a DVR, which digitizes and compresses the stream. IP cameras digitize video directly inside the camera sensor and transmit high-definition data packets over Ethernet Cat6 cables to an NVR, offering higher resolution potential, smart sensor analytics, and single-cable Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) convenience.
Storage retention duration depends on several technical variables: the number of connected cameras, video resolution (e.g., 2MP vs 4K), frame rate (fps), compression codec (e.g., H.264 vs H.265), whether recording is continuous or motion-triggered, and the total capacity of installed surveillance hard drives (e.g., 2TB vs 8TB).
Yes, modern DVR and NVR recording systems support secure remote viewing applications for smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers, provided the recorder is connected to an active local internet router with adequate upload bandwidth. Access is secured using encrypted credentials and authenticated user permissions.
Responsible camera planning positions lenses strictly aimed within your legal property boundaries. In scenarios where wider angles catch neighbouring windows or private courtyards, software privacy masking features are configured in the recorder to permanently blackout those specific screen quadrants.
