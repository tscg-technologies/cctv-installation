SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

HOW A CCTV SYSTEM WORKS

An illustrative overview of the technical pipeline from optical capture to video storage and authorized monitoring.

CENTRALIZED MONITORING HUB

SYNCHRONIZED SURVEILLANCE INFRASTRUCTURE

From edge camera sensors to local NVR storage drives and multi-display control consoles, every hardware component connects into a coherent, secure security network designed for continuous recording and instant playback.

● Multi-Screen Operations Center Architecture
01

Optical Capture

The camera sensor captures ambient light, converting visual scenes into high-definition digital or analog signals.

02

Signal Transmission

Video travels via shielded Cat6 network cabling, coaxial wiring, or encrypted local Wi-Fi frequencies.

03

Video Recording

A dedicated DVR or NVR processor synchronizes video feeds, applies compression (H.265), and tags motion events.

04

Data Storage

Surveillance-grade hard drives store video files securely for days or weeks based on capacity and recording profiles.

05

Authorized Access

Authorized users view live video feeds or inspect archived event recordings via HDMI monitor or secured mobile applications.

06

System Maintenance

Regular cleaning, connector integrity inspections, and firmware updates support continuous long-term operation.

PLANNING & BEST PRACTICES

WHY PLAN YOUR CCTV INSTALLATION?

Thoughtful design avoids blind spots, protects neighbor privacy, and optimizes equipment investment.

01

Property-Specific Planning

Every property features distinct perimeter lines, lighting conditions, and structural obstacles that demand individual camera positioning.

02

Suitable Camera Selection

Matching camera focal lengths, sensor resolutions, and ingress protection ensures hardware matches the exact outdoor or indoor setting.

03

Thoughtful Camera Placement

Carefully aimed viewing angles capture critical access points while respecting boundary lines and neighbor privacy considerations.

04

Installation & Maintenance Support

Tidy conduit cabling, secure power supplies, and periodic routine checkups prevent unexpected signal loss or recorder downtime.

Notice: A CCTV system provides visual monitoring and recorded visibility. It does not provide absolute crime prevention or guarantee security outcomes.
